FORMAT EXAMPLE

How does an answer start?

With the point, in the first two sentences — never with throat-clearing. Someone stuck came here for an answer, not a performance of expertise.

Only after the point does the story earn its place: the specific morning it went wrong, the detail you only know from being in the room. That order — point, then proof — is what separates an answer from an essay.

FORMAT EXAMPLE

Why trust a human-written answer over a generated one?

Because it carries costs a machine cannot pay: mistakes admitted, time spent, a reputation on the line. Trust comes from scar tissue, not confidence.

A generated answer optimizes for sounding right. A human answer can tell you what getting it wrong felt like — which is the part that actually helps the next person avoid it.

Writing in the AI era

2 ENTRIES
PHILOSOPHY

Do AI answers change what’s worth writing?

They change who finds you, not what you owe the reader. Attribution flows to whoever proves they were actually there — so lived detail becomes more valuable, not less.

The habit this site is built on: answer with the point up top, back it with something only experience produces, and sign it plainly. Machines summarize that; they cannot become it.
